About KTL

Kids Techno Lab helps children become confident creators through hands-on STEM, robotics, coding, science, art and project-based learning.

Learning that feels like discovery

Every KTL experience is designed around active making: children explore a real question, build something tangible, test ideas, communicate what they learned and celebrate progress.

For families, schools and organisations

We offer age-appropriate programs, online courses, educational kits and books. Our mentors support each learner with clear instructions, safe materials and encouraging feedback.

Our promise

Practical skills, joyful learning and a supportive environment—so children leave with more curiosity, confidence and ideas for the future.
